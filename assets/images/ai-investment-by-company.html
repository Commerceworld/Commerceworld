AI Infrastructure Investment by Company, 2023–2026

Annual capital expenditure (including finance leases) at the four biggest AI infrastructure spenders. This is real, reported spending — not an estimate — pulled from each company's own 10-K filings and earnings releases. 2026 figures are company guidance issued during 2026 earnings calls.

Sources: figures are calendar-year capital expenditures including finance lease principal payments, compiled from company SEC filings, earnings press releases, and cross-checked against Platformonomics' annual "Follow the CAPEX" hyperscaler retrospective.
  • Meta: 10-K / Q4 earnings releases (2023: $28.10B, 2024: $39.23B, 2025: $72.22B)
  • Alphabet/Google: Q4 earnings releases and 10-K filings (2023: $32.25B, 2024: $52.54B, 2025: $91.45B)
  • Amazon: 10-K filings and earnings releases (2023: $52.73B, 2024: $83.0B, 2025: $131.82B)
  • Microsoft: derived from combined 4-company annual totals reported by Platformonomics ($155B in 2023, $251B in 2024, ~$416B in 2025) minus the other three companies' confirmed figures, since Microsoft's fiscal year (ending June 30) doesn't map cleanly onto calendar-year press releases the way the other three do.
  • 2026 guidance: company earnings calls and CNBC/Yahoo Finance/Tom's Hardware coverage of Q1–Q2 2026 results (Microsoft ~$190B, Alphabet $175–185B, Amazon ~$200B, Meta $125–145B).
Figures round to the nearest $0.1B. Combined 4-company capex is on pace to approach $725B in 2026, up from roughly $155B just three years earlier in 2023.
